## Full description (4,000 characters max)

OpenRoad is free motorbike navigation for the UK and Europe, built on open map data. No account, no adverts, no subscription.

TWISTY ROUTES
• Four riding styles: Twisty, Extra twisty, No motorways and Fastest
• Up to three route options with miles, time, a bendiness score and how much is motorway
• Tap the map to add stops and via points, and drag them to reshape the route
• Round trips: a loop of the length and direction you choose
• Top rides: the best biking roads near any place

TURN-BY-TURN FOR THE HELMET
• Big arrows and spoken directions through your intercom or headset, with music turned down while it speaks
• Bends ahead spoken early with a speed to take them at
• Speed limit sign, speed camera warnings in the UK and Ireland, and automatic rerouting
• Voice commands: "find fuel", "how far to go", "tell the group…"

WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL
• Download country maps on Wi-Fi
• Plan routes and reroute with no signal, from the maps on your phone
• Saved routes keep their directions offline

PLAN WHOLE TRIPS
• Day-by-day tours with ferry and tunnel crossings, hotels, tolls and costs split per rider
• Weather for each day, drawn on the route for the time you'll be there: rain, gusts and icy stretches
• Fuel range from your tank size and mpg, with warnings for gaps too long for your tank
• Export as PDF, GPX or text, and import GPX routes and tracks

TODAY CARD AND WIDGET
• Is it a riding day? Dry hours, temperature, wind and daylight where you are
• A twisty loop from home ready to ride, on the app and on a home-screen widget

RIDE WITH FRIENDS
• Group rides: see each other on the map, chat, share routes and regroup points
• Everything is encrypted with your group's code
• Hazard warnings from other riders: gravel, diesel, a crash ahead

SAFETY
• Safety check-in: if you don't tap "I'm OK" in time, your phone alarms and your group is told
• Crash detection while you ride
• Emergency (ICE) card with your medical notes, shown full screen
• Tiredness and break reminders

YOUR RIDES
• Rides record themselves when you set off, and save when you park up
• Ride log with season totals, lean angle estimates, ride diary with photos
• 3D hills and routes that draw themselves on the map

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Road data can be out of date: follow road signs and the law.
